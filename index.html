Dear Ishuuu,

Today is your day, my princess 👑

Real flowers fade, so I made you a bouquet that blooms every single time you open it.

Every petal is a little wish for you: more laughter, softer days, and everything your heart dreams of. Never forget how special you are.

I wanted to come and greet you, but every time I ask, you say it may not work out that day. I felt a little sad about it, but I don’t want to ask too much and make things complicated, so I stopped asking to meet. I’m okay with it. Whenever you say it’s time, that’s when we’ll meet.

I miss you so much 😊

Happy Princess Day 💖
